# H-003 — AR foreground task with bounded secondary scan

**Hypothesis (not tested):** AR can retain several contextual representations and identify potentially useful institutional improvements while preserving accuracy, completion and budget of its **primary assignment**.

**Proposed method:** give the same source to a foreground-only baseline and a bounded dual-attention design; measure source fidelity, primary completion, compute consumption, proposal novelty, independently verified relevance and false improvements.

**Expected:** worthwhile, traceable candidates at constrained incremental cost. **Challenge:** loss of task fidelity, increased unsupported proposals, disproportionate cost or adoption without authorization.

**Governance:** all improvement candidates remain **proposal only**, subject to COO and relevant offices. Source 22.
