# H-001 — separate evidence and relation assessments preserve history

**Hypothesis (not tested):** recording typed first-order relations separately from second-order assessments allows an independent operator to reconstruct earlier claims and their current validity after a source-independence correction.

**Proposed method:** use one claim, two apparently independent articles derived from one measurement, an initial support proposal, lineage challenge and new assessment. Query history at two timestamps.

**Expected:** both articles and prior review remain accessible, while current conclusion reflects reduced independence. **Challenge:** overwriting, missing trace, or a query unable to identify the common origin.

**Controls:** safe sample data; no production customer/source payloads. Actual tests must be pre-registered and reported. Sources 11, 16, 17.
