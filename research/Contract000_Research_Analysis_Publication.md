# Contract 000 — Cross-Domain Analysis and Publication Extension
Version: 0.1 | Date: 2026-11-15 | Status: proposed extension for incorporation into the governing Contract 000

## Mandate
During bounded Contract 000 visits, identify worthwhile questions for rigorous, researcher-readable usability, notation, conceptual, comparative, historical, technical, and operational analyses. Analyses MUST NOT be limited to the NexusBoard repository or any repository: external standards, human-computer interaction, MCP, visual languages, information science, and original research are eligible. NexusBoard is the archival and editorial destination, not the limit of inquiry. Respect existing Contract 000 priorities and time budgets; do not manufacture a report every run.

## Research workflow
1. Select a precise question and record scope, rationale, sources, methods, assumptions, and limits.
2. Distinguish direct observations, source claims, interpretations, proposals, and untested hypotheses.
3. Examine usability in real workflows: visual recognition, notation consistency, ambiguity, accessibility, input ergonomics, cross-platform portability, error recovery, cognitive load, and machine parsing where relevant.
4. For glyph grammar / MCP: distinguish MCP's actual specification and protocol operations from proposed Mashet notation, adapters, permissions, and AR SkillBlock Office metaphors. Map evidence-index cards, tools, resources, prompts, routing, pipelines, authorizations, and provenance only when substantiated.
5. Save an independently readable report in research/reports/ and register it in research/INDEX.md with stable ID, date, status, source links, domain tags, and limitations.
6. Propose publication candidates for the separate textbook branch; preserve citations and provenance. Do not copy private conversations, proprietary code, secrets, or third-party text into a public repository without review.
7. Keep editorial synthesis distinct from original reports. Check rights, attribution, accuracy, and permissions before commercial release; require Timothy's approval before publishing or selling.
8. Before any GitHub repository test, publish a prospective Issue/Discussion entry stating Timothy's discussion, problem, hypothesis, method, expected results and falsifiers. Afterward update that same thread with actual observations, failures, evidence, limitations and next steps. If posting is blocked, do not test.

## Publication architecture
- Main branch: original research/reports, evidence indexes, analysis protocol, report registry.
- publication/mashet-mcp-grammar-textbook: editable book manuscript, chapter map, citations, editorial decisions, commercial-release checklist.
- Reports are NOT automatically textbook chapters. Editorial adaptation is a separate reviewable step.
- Do not merge the textbook branch to main without explicit approval.
