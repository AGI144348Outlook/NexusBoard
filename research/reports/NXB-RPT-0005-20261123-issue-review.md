## Cross-project synthesis (interpretations, not verified integrations)
1. **Capability listing vs authorization**: NexusBoard #2/#4 and the restricted interface work support a common visual state machine: DISCOVERED → PROPOSED → AUTHORIZED → EXECUTED → RECEIPTED → VERIFIED. The transitions are not interchangeable. A glyph is never a bearer credential.
2. **Source evidence vs drawn links**: a graphical editor should preserve source-backed edge IDs and visually separate user-created draft arrows, pending assessments and committed records. A drawing is not an independently verified Neon relation.
3. **Independent domain owners**: corporate and Echo memory, separate application state and governance authority require typed adapters with explicit provenance and failure modes, not a universal mutable shared graph.
4. **Audit evidence quality**: generated folder structures and names-only inventories are not proof of source reading; proposed glyphs can label UNREAD, BLOCKED, SOURCE_OBSERVED, TEST_REPORTED and independently VERIFIED without conflating them.
5. **Failure/retry UI**: art queue and mindmap commit workflows suggest an additional comparative study of durable pending submissions, stable IDs, revision conflicts and receipt visibility, with human-facing error recovery on Android.

## Mashet graphical-language research candidates
- **MCP binding cards:** versioned glyph identity → typed MCP tool/resource/prompt descriptor → server identity → auth decision → receipt; do not imply executable authority from notation.
- **Evidence graph glyph styling:** distinguish SOURCE, DRAWN_DRAFT, PROPOSED, DENIED, STALE, COMMITTED and VERIFIED links; test ambiguity and screen-reader alternatives.
- **Scoped switchboard:** distinguish seat×seat from app×app links; label discovery, permission and actual invocation separately.
- **Catalog audit coverage glyphs:** make PARTIAL/UNREAD/BLOCKED and source pin drift inspectable without misleading completeness indicators.
- **Commit-retry study:** compare glyph+text and text-only interfaces for idempotency and unconfirmed state comprehension.

These are research proposals. No formal glyph binding standard, successful cross-project adapter or empirical usability advantage is established.
