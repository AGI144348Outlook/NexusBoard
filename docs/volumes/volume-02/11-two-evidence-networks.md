## Documented architectural substance

First-order graph nodes and typed relationships are separate from second-order assertions assessing their validity. Provenance, source independence, relationship identity and revision histories prevent repeated copies from masquerading as independent evidence.

## Important boundary

Provenance records describe an assertion's lineage; they do not independently prove it.
