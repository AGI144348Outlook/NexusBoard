## Documented architectural substance

PostgreSQL privileges, constitutional Agent duties and evidentiary roles are three independent meanings of role. Role proposals themselves become evidence requiring approval; least privilege and RLS constrain database access.

## Important boundary

Do not create thousands of database login roles for contextual hypotheses or allow self-approved grants.
