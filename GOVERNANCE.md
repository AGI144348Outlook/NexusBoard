# Governance and decision rights

NexusBoard models one System Entity with enduring constitutional authority despite model and operator turnover. The model may recommend; **server-enforced governance and human decisions** control reserved actions.

## Authority levels

Owner → versioned corporate constitution → delegated department → seat charter → assigned Agent → temporary operator lease. Each downstream scope can narrow but not elevate the original authority.

## Proposed governance path

A seat such as AR proposes an evidence-backed change. COO classifies impact and requests relevant CTO, CDO, CKO, CFO, CRO, CISO, R&D or legal/compliance reviews. Competent CEO/Board/owner authority approves reserved effects. Implementation receives its own verified outcome. Independent Audit can inspect the proposal, objections, evaluation and actual effect. Every decision retains timestamp, charter version, authority principal and evidence references.

## Separation of duties

No Agent self-approves privilege gains or a contested link. CDO protects structural data integrity, not the meaning of every claim. AR proposes contextual relationships, not global automatic adoption. Audit does not silently modify its own independent findings. An operator session is never the legal owner or constitutional identity.

## Records

Proposal ID; alternatives; source/version; proposer; applicable jurisdiction; decision authority; reviewer observations and objections; approve/defer/reject result; effective time; rollback/appeal route; implementation evidence and audit links.
