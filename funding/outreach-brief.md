# Targeted collaboration outreach brief

**Status:** sample communication framework, not evidence that contacts or solicitations have occurred.

## Audience-specific relevance

- Agent platform teams: identity/state independent of replaceable model operators.
- MCP implementers: relative seat autonomy and protocol-vs-authority distinction.
- PostgreSQL/knowledge-graph researchers: dual evidence networks, relation provenance and non-destructive review.
- Security/evaluation groups: role boundaries, independent assessment and audit.
- Small software agencies: paid pilot architecture or technical specification work.

## Message outline

Introduce the project as **proposed and source-backed R&D**, link [Architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md), describe one problem relevant to the recipient, identify the smallest paid pilot or technical review request, specify current evidence and limitations, and ask whether the scope matches their interest.

No statements of proven commercial outcomes, previously completed client work, or deployed twenty-seat operation without verification. Record outreach date, organization/contact consent, requested action, response and next steps in a private CRM, **not** a public issue containing personal information.
