# Sponsorship and collaboration

NexusBoard welcomes conversations about **research sponsorship**, narrowly scoped **paid technical pilots**, and contributions from teams working on multi-agent architecture, evidence provenance, MCP interoperability, agent-state continuity, PostgreSQL or governance.

## What this is

An **early-stage architectural R&D effort** with 25 numbered source notes and a proposed development roadmap. It is **not** a completed commercial product, a verified twenty-agent deployment, or an investment offer.

## Useful pilot types

1. Evidence and relation-assessment graph with time-aware reconstruction.
2. Authenticated MCP gateway and two independently authorized seat services.
3. Persistent Agent checkpoint and lease-bound operator handoff.
4. Independent technical review of safety/permission and cost tradeoffs.

Each inquiry should establish deliverables, measures, confidentiality, data rights, budget and funding terms *before* execution. Sponsor opinions and results should not bypass ordinary evidence review.

## Contact and funding routes

No verified sponsorship/payment URL, public contact email or contracting entity has been supplied for publication; **do not invent one**. Use GitHub Issues for nonconfidential project-interest proposals until a contact process is approved. Do not post private deal terms or sensitive data publicly.
