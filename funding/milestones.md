# NexusBoard development funding milestones

**Planning only. No donations received, budget approved or sponsorship agreement claimed by this file.** Sponsorship should support a bounded deliverable rather than imply that the entire architecture is implemented.

## Example milestone sequence

| Milestone | Deliverable | Evidence of completion | Funding status |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-0 — documentation | Accurate public architecture, source notes, licensing and privacy review | versioned repository, reviewed status ledger | Unfunded / documentation in progress |
| F-1 — governance/data pilot | Narrow relational evidence slice with source, edge, assessment and revision | reviewed schema/method, recorded pre/post test evidence | Proposed |
| F-2 — operator/seat demo | Two authorized seat services, one bounded mission, handoff and denial case | audited capability trace and reproduction guide | Proposed |
| F-3 — public technical report | Accessibility review, diagrams, limitations, performance/cost report | linked external review and reproducibility evidence | Proposed |

Actual requested dollar amounts must follow scoped estimates for engineering time, service charges, independent verification, support, licensing and contingency. Never promise specific dates or return on investment without an approved contract.

## Sponsorship guardrails

Do not offer equity, revenue share or financial returns through an informal crowdfunding reward. Describe sponsor visibility, artifact license and IP rights precisely. Keep client-funded deliverables separate from general open R&D. Define how funding is treated if a milestone is delayed or impossible.
