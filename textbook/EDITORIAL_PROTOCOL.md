# Editorial and Commercial Publication Controls

1. Assign every claim a source ID, evidence type, confidence/status, and chapter location.
2. Preserve raw research reports separately from edited prose; never silently turn hypotheses into results.
3. Reconcile against current MCP specifications and date every standards-dependent claim.
4. Check originality, third-party quotation and image rights, license compatibility, privacy, and attribution.
5. Avoid reproducing private conversations or unapproved confidential material in this public repository.
6. Conduct independent copyediting, technical review, and accessibility review before release.
7. Require Timothy's explicit sign-off on title, authorship, manuscript, pricing, licensing, and publication platform.
8. Keep revisions and release candidates versioned; never automatically publish or sell.
